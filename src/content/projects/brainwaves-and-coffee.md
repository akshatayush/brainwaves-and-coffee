This site is the project. Meta, I know.

## Why I built it

I had thoughts that were too long for social media and too informal for anything else. I needed a place to put them. A corner of the internet that felt *mine* — not algorithmically optimized, not chasing engagement, just a place to write and think and share.

## What I used

- **[Astro](https://astro.build)** for the framework — generates static HTML, fast, zero JS shipped to the browser by default
- **Markdown** for posts — just drop a `.md` file to publish something new
- **Google Fonts** — Playfair Display and Lora for the warm, editorial feel
- **Netlify** for hosting — free tier, auto-deploys whenever I push to GitHub

## What I wanted

The design brief I gave myself: cozy. Like a good coffee shop where you can sit for three hours and nobody bothers you. Warm colors, readable typography, plenty of whitespace.

No dark mode toggle. No comment section. No analytics. Just words.

## What I learned

Astro is genuinely pleasant to work with. Content collections make it trivially easy to add new posts. The whole build process is snappy. If you want a personal site and you're comfortable with a little bit of HTML/CSS, it's the right choice.
